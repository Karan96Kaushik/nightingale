## Day 1 — Greetings & introductions

**Vocabulary:** greetings, introductions, numbers 1–20  
**Grammar:** soy and me llamo  
**Listen:** beginner Spanish pronunciation  
**Practice:** say your name, where you are from, and what you do

### Grammar

**Two ways to say who you are.** Soy means “I am” for origin and job. Me llamo means “my name is”. A question starts with the question word; the rest stays in the same order.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Soy de Londres. | I am from London. |
| Me llamo Alex. | My name is Alex. |
| ¿Cómo te llamas? | What is your name? |
| ¿De dónde eres? | Where are you from? |

Not yet: the other forms of ser come on day 3, after es is used for the day and the time. Gender and vosotros wait until a phrase needs them.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hola, me llamo Alex. | Hi, my name is Alex. | |
| ¿Cómo te llamas? | What is your name? | |
| Mucho gusto. | Nice to meet you. | |
| Soy de Londres. | I am from London. | |
| ¿De dónde eres? | Where are you from? | |
| Trabajo como ingeniero de software. | I work as a software engineer. | |
| ¿Cómo estás? | How are you? | |
| Estoy bien, gracias. ¿Y tú? | I am well, thank you. And you? | |
| Buenos días. | Good morning. | |
| Buenas tardes. | Good afternoon. | |
| Buenas noches. | Good evening / good night. | |
| Hasta luego. | See you later. | |
| Tengo veintinueve años. | I am twenty-nine years old. | Numbers 1–20: uno, dos, tres… veinte. |
| Uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco. | One, two, three, four, five. | |

### Dialogue — Meeting someone new

- **Ana:** ¡Hola! ¿Cómo te llamas? — Hi! What is your name?
- **Alex:** Me llamo Alex. ¿Y tú? — My name is Alex. And you?
- **Ana:** Me llamo Ana. Mucho gusto. — My name is Ana. Nice to meet you.
- **Alex:** Encantado. Soy de Londres. — Nice to meet you. I am from London.
- **Ana:** Yo soy de Madrid. ¿A qué te dedicas? — I am from Madrid. What do you do?
- **Alex:** Trabajo como ingeniero. ¿Y tú? — I work as an engineer. And you?
- **Ana:** Soy profesora. Hasta luego. — I am a teacher. See you later.

### Practice — Introduce yourself

Say your name, where you are from, and what you do. Keep going even if you stumble.

Starters: *Hola, me llamo…* / *Soy de…* / *Vivo en…* / *Trabajo como… / Soy estudiante.* / *Mucho gusto.*

Speak out loud. Do not stop to translate every word. Repeat the whole introduction three times.

## Day 2 — Days, months & time

**Vocabulary:** days, months, and the clock  
**Grammar:** es la una, son las…  
**Listen:** a slow dialogue about the day and the time  
**Practice:** say the greeting, the day, the date, and the time

### Grammar

**One o’clock is singular.** Es means “it is” for the day and for one o’clock: es la una. Every other hour is son las. Y media is half past, and menos cuarto is a quarter to. On the clock, say de la tarde. For a plan, say por la tarde. Days and months are not capitalised. Day 3 uses this same es for people.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hoy es lunes. | Today is Monday. |
| Es la una. | It is one o’clock. |
| Son las tres y media. | It is half past three. |
| Son las siete de la tarde. | It is seven in the evening. |

Not yet: estar (estoy, está, estamos). Minutes other than y media and menos cuarto. People with es come on day 3.

### Days

lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo

### Months

enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre, octubre, noviembre, diciembre

### Clock numbers

One o’clock uses **una**, not uno. The other hours: dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once, doce.

- **Es la una.** It is one o’clock.
- **Son las tres.** It is three o’clock.
- **Son las tres y media.** It is half past three.
- **Son las diez menos cuarto.** It is a quarter to ten.
- **Son las siete de la tarde.** It is seven in the evening. (clock)
- **Por la tarde.** In the afternoon. (plans)
- **A las diez.** At ten.

**Date pattern:** el + number + de + month. *Hoy es el doce de septiembre.* Today is the twelfth of September.

**Mañana** means tomorrow. **Por la mañana** means in the morning.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Hoy es lunes. | Today is Monday. | Days are not capitalised. |
| Mañana es martes. | Tomorrow is Tuesday. | Mañana = tomorrow. Por la mañana = in the morning. |
| ¿Qué día es hoy? | What day is it today? | |
| ¿Qué mes es? Es septiembre. | What month is it? It is September. | Months are not capitalised. |
| ¿Qué hora es? | What time is it? | |
| Es la una. | It is one o’clock. | Una, not uno. |
| Son las tres. | It is three o’clock. | |
| Son las tres y media. | It is half past three. | |
| Son las diez menos cuarto. | It is a quarter to ten. | |
| Por la mañana. | In the morning. | |
| Por la tarde. | In the afternoon. | Plans. The clock uses de la tarde. |
| Por la noche. | At night. | |
| ¿Cuál es la fecha de hoy? | What is today’s date? | |
| Hoy es el doce de septiembre. | Today is the twelfth of September. | el + number + de + month. |

### Dialogue — Making plans

- **Luis:** Buenos días. ¿Qué día es hoy? — Good morning. What day is it today?
- **Marta:** Hoy es miércoles. — Today is Wednesday.
- **Luis:** ¿Qué hora es? — What time is it?
- **Marta:** Son las siete de la tarde. — It is seven in the evening.
- **Luis:** ¿El sábado por la mañana? — Saturday morning?
- **Marta:** Sí. A las diez. — Yes. At ten.

### Practice — Time and date

Greet for this part of the day, then say today’s day, the date, and the time. Add one plan for the weekend.

Starters: *Buenos días. / Buenas tardes. / Buenas noches.* / *Hoy es…* / *Hoy es el … de …* / *Es la una. / Son las…* / *El sábado por la mañana…*

One o’clock is es la una. Every other hour is son las. Days and months are not capitalised.

## Day 3 — Ser — who and what

**Vocabulary:** ser: soy, eres, es, somos, son  
**Grammar:** ser for who and what  
**Listen:** a beginner dialogue using ser  
**Practice:** say who you are, then who someone else is

### Grammar

**Five forms, no article before a job.** Day 1 was soy and me llamo. Hoy es from day 2 is the same es. Ser is for who someone is, where they are from, and a lasting quality. Say soy, eres, es, somos, son. Do not put un or una before a job. Put no before the verb to negate. Use -o for a man and -a for a woman on these jobs: ingeniero, ingeniera, profesor, profesora.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Soy ingeniero. | I am an engineer. |
| ¿Eres de Londres? | Are you from London? |
| Ella es profesora. | She is a teacher. |
| No soy médico. | I am not a doctor. |

| Person | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| yo | soy | Soy ingeniero. |
| tú | eres | ¿Eres de Londres? |
| él / ella | es | Ella es profesora. Hoy es lunes. |
| nosotros | somos | Somos de Londres. |
| ellos / ellas | son | Ellos son profesores. |

Not yet: vosotros (sois) and estar. You will hear sois in Spain; you do not need to say it. Gender beyond these job endings waits until a later phrase needs it.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Soy ingeniero. | I am an engineer. | No un or una before a job. Same fact as trabajo como ingeniero from day 1. |
| ¿Eres de Londres? | Are you from London? | |
| Tú eres muy amable. | You are very kind. | |
| Ella es de España. | She is from Spain. | |
| Ella es profesora. | She is a teacher. | Profesora for a woman, profesor for a man. Ingeniera and ingeniero work the same way. |
| Nosotros somos amigos. | We are friends. | |
| Ellos son profesores. | They are teachers. | |
| ¿Eres ingeniero? | Are you an engineer? | |
| No soy médico. | I am not a doctor. | |
| Somos de Londres. | We are from London. | |
| ¿Quién es ella? | Who is she? | |
| Es mi amiga. | She is my friend. | |
| Hoy es lunes y ella es Ana. | Today is Monday and she is Ana. | Es is the same word for the day and for a person. |

### Dialogue — Who is who

- **Pablo:** ¡Hola! Me llamo Pablo. ¿Cómo te llamas? — Hi! My name is Pablo. What is your name?
- **Sofía:** Me llamo Sofía. ¿Eres de aquí? — My name is Sofía. Are you from here?
- **Pablo:** Sí, soy de aquí. ¿Y tú? — Yes, I am from here. And you?
- **Sofía:** No, soy de Valencia. Soy profesora. — No, I am from Valencia. I am a teacher.
- **Pablo:** Yo soy ingeniero. ¿Quién es él? — I am an engineer. Who is he?
- **Sofía:** Es mi amigo. Somos amigos. — He is my friend. We are friends.

### Practice — Who you are

Introduce yourself again: name, origin, and job with soy. Then say who one other person is, using es. Aim for ten short sentences.

Starters: *Hola, me llamo…* / *Soy de…* / *Soy…* / *¿Eres…?* / *Ella es… / Él es…* / *Somos…* / *No soy…*

Ser is for identity, origin, and lasting qualities. No un or una before a job. Hoy es and ella es are the same es.

## Day 4 — Core verbs

**Vocabulary:** tener, querer, ir, hacer  
**Grammar:** tengo, quiero, voy, hago  
**Listen:** listen for these four verbs  
**Practice:** create two sentences with each verb

### Grammar

**Only the forms in today’s phrases.** You can already say who you are and what time it is. These four verbs are chunks, not a full table. Tengo is what you have, including hunger and age. Tengo que means have to. Quiero and voy are followed by a or another verb. The you-forms you will hear are tienes, quieres, and haces. Vamos and hacemos are we.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Tengo hambre. Tengo que trabajar. | I am hungry. I have to work. |
| Quiero café. ¿Qué quieres comer? | I want coffee. What do you want to eat? |
| Voy al trabajo. ¿Adónde vas? | I am going to work. Where are you going? |
| Hago ejercicio. ¿Qué haces hoy? | I exercise. What are you doing today? |

| Verb | I | You | We |
|---|---|---|---|
| tener (have) | tengo | tienes | |
| querer (want) | quiero | quieres | |
| ir (go) | voy | vas | vamos |
| hacer (do / make) | hago | haces | hacemos |

These four stay chunks. They do not follow the regular pattern on day 10.

Not yet: the rest of each conjugation, and the past tense. Leave vas, quieres, and haces as the you-forms in these phrases.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Tengo hambre. | I am hungry. | |
| ¿Tienes tiempo? | Do you have time? | Tienes is the you-form of tengo. |
| Quiero café. | I want coffee. | |
| ¿Qué quieres comer? | What do you want to eat? | |
| Voy al trabajo. | I am going to work. | |
| ¿Adónde vas? | Where are you going? | |
| Hago ejercicio por la mañana. | I exercise in the morning. | Por la mañana is the day 2 phrase. |
| ¿Qué haces hoy? | What are you doing today? | |
| No tengo dinero. | I do not have money. | |
| Quiero ir a casa. | I want to go home. | |
| Vamos al parque. | We are going to the park. | |
| Hacemos la cena juntos. | We make dinner together. | |
| Tengo que trabajar. | I have to work. | |

### Dialogue — Evening plans

- **Noa:** ¿Qué quieres hacer esta noche? — What do you want to do tonight?
- **Diego:** Tengo hambre. Quiero comer. — I am hungry. I want to eat.
- **Noa:** Vamos a un restaurante. — Let’s go to a restaurant.
- **Diego:** No tengo mucho tiempo. Tengo que trabajar mañana. — I do not have much time. I have to work tomorrow.
- **Noa:** Entonces hacemos algo rápido. — Then let’s do something quick.
- **Diego:** Perfecto. Voy a casa a las nueve. — Perfect. I am going home at nine.

### Practice — Two sentences each

Make two sentences with tener, two with querer, two with ir, and two with hacer. Use today’s time of day in one of them.

Starters: *Tengo… / No tengo…* / *Quiero… / ¿Qué quieres…?* / *Voy a… / Vamos a…* / *Hago… / ¿Qué haces…?*

Tener is used for hunger, age, and obligation (tener que). Keep the sentences short and spoken, not written first.

## Day 5 — Food & drinks

**Vocabulary:** food and drinks  
**Grammar:** un, una, and quiero  
**Listen:** ordering at a restaurant  
**Practice:** pretend to order a meal in Spanish

### Grammar

**Enough grammar to order.** Quiero from day 4 takes a thing or a verb. Un goes before a masculine noun and una before a feminine one: un café, una ensalada. Staff may say desea or recomienda. Answer with quiero. Soy vegetariano uses ser from day 3, with -o or -a like ingeniero.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Un café. Una paella. | A coffee. A paella. |
| Quiero comer. | I want to eat. |
| Para mí, una ensalada. | For me, a salad. |
| Soy vegetariano. | I am vegetarian. |

Not yet: gustar, object pronouns, and the usted verb table. Está delicioso is one fixed sentence, not a reason to study estar.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Quiero comer. | I want to eat. | |
| Una mesa para dos, por favor. | A table for two, please. | |
| La carta, por favor. | The menu, please. | |
| Para mí, una paella. | For me, a paella. | |
| Quiero un café con leche. | I want a coffee with milk. | |
| ¿Qué recomienda? | What do you recommend? | A fixed question for staff. Answer their next question with quiero. |
| Está delicioso. | It is delicious. | Say the whole sentence. The estar table comes later. |
| La cuenta, por favor. | The bill, please. | |
| Sin gluten, por favor. | Gluten-free, please. | |
| Agua con gas. | Sparkling water. | |
| Un vaso de vino tinto. | A glass of red wine. | |
| ¿Puedo pedir el postre? | Can I order dessert? | |
| Soy vegetariano. | I am vegetarian. | Vegetariana if you use the -a ending, like profesora. |

### Dialogue — At the restaurant

- **Camarero:** Buenas tardes. ¿Mesa para cuántos? — Good afternoon. A table for how many?
- **Alex:** Para uno, por favor. — For one, please.
- **Camarero:** ¿Qué desea beber? — What would you like to drink?
- **Alex:** Agua y un café, por favor. — Water and a coffee, please.
- **Camarero:** ¿Y para comer? — And to eat?
- **Alex:** Quiero una tortilla y una ensalada. — I want an omelette and a salad.
- **Camarero:** Muy bien. Enseguida. — Very good. Right away.

### Practice — Order a meal

Role-play walking into a restaurant, sitting down, ordering food and drink, then asking for the bill.

Starters: *Hola, una mesa para uno, por favor.* / *¿Qué recomienda?* / *Para mí…* / *Quiero…* / *La cuenta, por favor.*

Por favor and gracias do a lot of work. If you freeze, point at the menu and say *esto, por favor.*

## Day 6 — Family & people

**Vocabulary:** family and people  
**Grammar:** mi, mis, and matching endings  
**Listen:** a simple family description  
**Practice:** describe your family or friends

### Grammar

**One owner word, then match the ending.** Mi is for one person or thing. Mis is for more than one. Tengo from day 4 says how many people you have. Es and son from day 3 say who they are. The adjective follows the noun and matches it: -o or -os for masculine, -a for feminine. Vivo and vive stay whole chunks today.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Mi hermana. Mis amigos. | My sister. My friends. |
| Mis amigos son simpáticos. | My friends are nice. |
| Somos una familia pequeña. | We are a small family. |
| Mi padre es médico. | My father is a doctor. |

Not yet: su, nuestro, and tu. You may hear *¿dónde vive tu familia?* Answer with mi and mis. The present-tense pattern for vivo and vive comes later.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Esta es mi familia. | This is my family. | |
| Tengo un hermano y una hermana. | I have a brother and a sister. | |
| Mi madre se llama… | My mother’s name is… | Se llama is the same idea as me llamo from day 1. |
| Mi padre es médico. | My father is a doctor. | |
| Mis amigos son muy simpáticos. | My friends are very nice. | |
| Vivo con mi pareja. | I live with my partner. | Learn vivo as a chunk. The vivir pattern comes later. |
| No tengo hijos. | I do not have children. | |
| Mi abuela vive en la India. | My grandmother lives in India. | |
| Somos una familia pequeña. | We are a small family. | |
| Él es mi mejor amigo. | He is my best friend. | |
| Ella es muy inteligente. | She is very intelligent. | |
| ¿Tienes hermanos? | Do you have siblings? | |

### Dialogue — Talking about family

- **Elena:** ¿Tienes hermanos? — Do you have siblings?
- **Alex:** Sí, tengo una hermana. ¿Y tú? — Yes, I have a sister. And you?
- **Elena:** Tengo dos hermanos. Somos tres. — I have two brothers. There are three of us.
- **Alex:** ¿Dónde vive tu familia? — Where does your family live?
- **Elena:** Mis padres viven en Sevilla. Yo vivo en Londres. — My parents live in Seville. I live in London.

### Practice — Describe your people

Talk about 3–4 people: names, where they live, what they do, and one quality. Use mi or mis, then es or son.

Starters: *Esta es mi familia.* / *Tengo…* / *Mi… se llama…* / *Es… / Son…* / *Vive en…*

Use ser for jobs and personality. If you do not want to talk about family, describe friends instead.

## Day 7 — Week one review

**Review day.** Revise days 1–6. Speak for 5 minutes entirely in Spanish.

### Grammar

**Nothing new.** Reuse only what you can already say. Soy and no soy for who you are. Es la una and son las for the time. Tengo, quiero, voy, and hago for what you have, want, and do. Mi and mis, then a matching ending, for people. If a sentence needs anything else, use a chunk you know.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Soy… / No soy… | Identity and negation. |
| Es la una. Son las… | Telling the time. |
| Tengo… Quiero… Voy… Hago… | The four core verbs. |
| Mi hermana. Mis amigos son… | Possession and agreement. |

Not yet: any new tense or verb. Today is for speaking, not a new chart.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Hola, me llamo… y soy de… | Hi, my name is… and I am from… |
| Hoy es… y son las… | Today is… and it is… |
| Soy ingeniero. No soy médico. | I am an engineer. I am not a doctor. |
| Tengo hambre y quiero comer. | I am hungry and I want to eat. |
| Voy al restaurante con mis amigos. | I am going to the restaurant with my friends. |
| ¿Qué quieres beber? | What do you want to drink? |
| Mi hermana es profesora. | My sister is a teacher. |
| Somos de Londres. | We are from London. |
| Hago ejercicio por la mañana. | I exercise in the morning. |
| Hasta luego. El sábado por la mañana. | See you later. Saturday morning. |

### Dialogue — A full beginner conversation

- **Ana:** ¡Hola! ¿Cómo estás? — Hi! How are you?
- **Alex:** Estoy bien. Hoy es domingo. Quiero ir a un café. — I am well. Today is Sunday. I want to go to a café.
- **Ana:** Yo también. Tengo hambre. — Me too. I am hungry.
- **Alex:** Vamos. ¿Qué quieres comer? — Let’s go. What do you want to eat?
- **Ana:** Una tostada y un café con leche. — Toast and a coffee with milk.
- **Alex:** Perfecto. Mi hermana vive cerca. Es muy simpática. — Perfect. My sister lives nearby. She is very nice.

### Practice — Five minutes in Spanish

Speak for five minutes without switching to English. Introduce yourself, then today, food, and family.

Starters: *Hola, me llamo Alex. Soy de Londres…* / *Hoy es… Ahora son las…* / *Quiero… Tengo… Voy a…* / *Mi familia… Mis amigos…*

Do not stop when you make a mistake. Keep speaking. If you get stuck, repeat a phrase you already know.

## Day 8 — Places & directions

**Vocabulary:** places and directions  
**Grammar:** está for location  
**Listen:** a beginner directions dialogue  
**Practice:** explain how to get from home to a place

### Grammar

**Where something is.** Ser says who you are. Está says where a place is. Voy from day 4 plus en names the transport: voy en metro. Voy andando is on foot. Siga and gire are fixed direction phrases. Say them whole. Do not build an imperative chart.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Está a la derecha. | It is on the right. |
| Está cerca de aquí. | It is near here. |
| Voy en metro. | I go by metro. |
| Voy andando. | I go on foot. |

Not yet: the full estar table. Feelings come on day 11. Do not study the imperative.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Dónde está la estación? | Where is the station? | |
| Está a la derecha. | It is on the right. | |
| Está a la izquierda. | It is on the left. | |
| Siga todo recto. | Go straight ahead. | A fixed direction phrase. Do not change the ending. |
| Está cerca de aquí. | It is near here. | |
| Está lejos. | It is far. | |
| Gire en la segunda calle. | Turn at the second street. | Another fixed direction phrase, like siga. |
| El parque está al lado del café. | The park is next to the café. | |
| Voy en metro. | I go by metro. | |
| Voy andando. | I go on foot. | |
| ¿Cómo llego al centro? | How do I get to the centre? | Learn the question as one chunk. |
| Vivo cerca de la estación. | I live near the station. | Vivo is still the day 6 chunk. |

### Dialogue — Asking for directions

- **Alex:** Perdone, ¿dónde está el museo? — Excuse me, where is the museum?
- **Local:** Siga todo recto y gire a la izquierda. — Go straight and turn left.
- **Alex:** ¿Está lejos? — Is it far?
- **Local:** No, está cerca. Diez minutos andando. — No, it is close. Ten minutes on foot.
- **Alex:** Muchas gracias. — Thank you very much.
- **Local:** De nada. ¡Buen día! — You’re welcome. Have a good day!

### Practice — From home to a place

Explain how you get from home to the station, a café, or work. Use está for the place and voy for how you travel.

Starters: *Voy andando. / Voy en metro.* / *Siga todo recto.* / *Está a la derecha.* / *Está cerca de aquí.* / *Vivo cerca de…*

Use está for location, not soy. Keep it to 6–8 spoken sentences.

## Day 9 — Daily routine

**Vocabulary:** daily routine  
**Grammar:** me levanto and me gusta  
**Listen:** someone describing their day  
**Practice:** describe your own daily routine

### Grammar

**Say the routine as chunks.** Put the day in order with the clock from day 2: a las siete, a la una, por la mañana, por la tarde. Me plus the verb is the I-form for these routines: me levanto, me ducho, me acuesto. Me gusta takes one thing or a verb. Voy, hago, and trabajo stay the chunks you already use.

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| Me levanto. Me ducho. Me acuesto. | I get up. I shower. I go to bed. |
| Me gusta correr. | I like to run. |
| Primero desayuno. Después trabajo. | First I have breakfast. Then I work. |
| Me levanto a las siete. | I get up at seven. |

Not yet: te and se, me gustan, and the full reflexive table. Trabajas is a you-form you may hear; the ending pattern is day 10.

### Phrases

| Spanish | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Me levanto a las siete. | I get up at seven. | |

## Day 11 — Estar — place and feeling

**Vocabulary:** estar: estoy, estás, está…  
**Grammar:** ser or estar  
**Listen:** listen for ser vs estar  
**Practice:** describe where you are and how you feel

### Grammar

**Who you are, or where and how you are now.** Ser is still identity and origin: soy ingeniero, soy de Londres. Day 8 used está for where a place is. Today the same verb has five forms for people: estoy, estás, está, estamos, están. Use them for where someone is and how they feel right now. Estoy bien from day 1 is this pattern. Cansado or cansada matches you, the same -o or -a agreement as day 6.

| Spanish | English |

| Soy ingeniero. | I am an engineer. |
| Estoy en casa. | I am at home. |
| Estoy cansado. | I am tired. |

## Reference — patterns from the two weeks

### Ser and estar

| | Ser (who / what) | Estar (where / how now) |
|---|---|---|
| yo | soy | estoy |
| tú | eres | estás |
| él / ella | es | está |
| nosotros | somos | estamos |
| ellos / ellas | son | están |

- Ser: identity, origin, job, lasting quality. No un or una before a job. *Soy ingeniero. Soy de Londres. Ella es profesora.*
- Es is also the day and one o’clock: *Hoy es lunes. Es la una.*
- Estar: location and current feeling. *Estoy en casa. Estoy cansado. El café está aquí.*
- Negation: **no** before the verb. *No soy médico. No tengo dinero. No estoy ocupado. No fui a casa temprano.*

### Regular present (day 10)

Subject pronouns are optional. Questions do not add “do”.

| | yo | tú | él / ella | nosotros | ellos / ellas |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| -ar | -o | -as | -a | -amos | -an |
| -er | -o | -es | -e | -emos | -en |
| -ir | -o | -es | -e | -imos | -en |

hablar → hablo, hablas, habla, hablamos, hablan  
comer → como, comes, come, comemos, comen  
vivir → vivo, vives, vive, vivimos, viven

Also regular in these lessons: trabajar, estudiar, beber, escribir, desayunar, cenar, llegar.

Se llama is the same idea as me llamo.

### Articles, possession, agreement

- **un** before a masculine noun, **una** before a feminine one: un café, una ensalada, un hermano, una hermana.
- **mi** for one, **mis** for more than one.
- Adjectives follow the noun and match: -o / -os masculine, -a feminine. *Mis amigos son simpáticos. Estoy cansado / cansada.*
- Job endings: ingeniero / ingeniera, profesor / profesora, vegetariano / vegetariana.

### Time, days, months

Days (not capitalised): lunes, martes, miércoles, jueves, viernes, sábado, domingo.

Months (not capitalised): enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre, octubre, noviembre, diciembre.

- *¿Qué día es hoy? ¿Qué hora es? ¿Qué mes es? ¿Cuál es la fecha de hoy?*
- *Es la una.* Every other hour: *son las…*
- *y media* = half past. *menos cuarto* = a quarter to.
- Clock: *de la tarde*. Plans: *por la mañana / por la tarde / por la noche*.
- Date: *el + number + de + month*. *Hoy es el doce de septiembre.*
- Numbers named in the lessons: uno–cinco, and clock hours dos–doce, plus doce and veinte. *Tengo veintinueve años* is the age chunk.
